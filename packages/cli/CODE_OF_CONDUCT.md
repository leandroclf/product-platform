# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and exclusionary behavior are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make space for newcomers and explain assumptions.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and provider terms.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation are prohibited in project spaces and when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainers' contact methods exposed by GitHub. Do not use public issues when disclosure could harm a participant. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants according to severity and pattern of behavior.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize safety, confidentiality, proportionality, and a clear record of the behavior involved. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
